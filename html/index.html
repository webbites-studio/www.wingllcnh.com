Bridgerton Garden Experience

Step into an evening of elegance, connection, and inspiration

Step into an evening of elegance, connection, and inspiration at our exclusive Bridgerton Garden Experience — a refined social soirée designed for ambitious, stylish, and inspiring women. Set within a beautifully curated private venue in Manchester, NH, this immersive experience blends luxury, creativity, and meaningful networking in an atmosphere inspired by the charm of an English royal garden.

What to expect: • ✨ Exquisite Bridgerton-inspired décor and ambiance • 🧵 A sophisticated accessories workshop presented by our featured partner, Raisa’s Sewing & Accessories • 🥂 Champagne and elegant light refreshments • 📸 A beautifully styled photo experience • 🎻 Romantic music and an intimate high-society atmosphere • 🤝 Meaningful conversations and connections with like-minded women.

Dress Code: Elegant & Chic — timeless glamour, pearls, flowing dresses, and refined sophistication. Embrace your inner queen with fascinators, elegant tea hats, lace gloves, satin gloves, and vintage-inspired accessories.

Tickets: Early Access — $65 (through June 3); General Admission — $95 (after June 3). Location details shared with confirmed attendees only.
